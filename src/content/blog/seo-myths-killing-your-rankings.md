## You've Been Lied To About SEO

There's more bad SEO advice on the internet than good advice. And most businesses are following the bad stuff — because it sounds right, it's easy to find, and nobody's told them otherwise.

Until now.

## Myth 1: "Just Blog More"

Publishing three blogs a week that nobody reads isn't a strategy. It's a content graveyard. One excellent, well-optimised piece will outperform ten mediocre ones every time.

Quality beats quantity. Always.

## Myth 2: "Keywords Are Everything"

Keywords matter, but they're not magic. Google's algorithm is smarter than you think. It cares about intent, relevance, and whether your content actually answers the question someone's asking.

Stuffing "best marketing agency Brisbane" into every paragraph isn't going to cut it.

## Myth 3: "SEO Is Free Traffic"

SEO takes time, expertise, and consistent effort. It's an investment — and a good one — but calling it "free" sets the wrong expectations. The businesses that win at SEO are the ones that commit to it for the long haul.

## Myth 4: "My Web Developer Handles SEO"

No offence to developers, but building a website and optimising it for search are two completely different skills. A fast, well-coded site is the foundation — but SEO strategy, content, and link building require specialist knowledge.

## Myth 5: "I'll See Results in a Month"

If someone promises you first-page rankings in 30 days, run. Real SEO takes 3-6 months minimum to show meaningful results. Anyone telling you otherwise is either lying or using tactics that will get you penalised.

## What Actually Works

Consistent, strategic, long-term SEO built on solid technical foundations, quality content, and genuine authority in your space.
