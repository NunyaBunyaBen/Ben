## The Brief

Dara is a wellness expert who was coaching a group at a conference on stretching and breathing exercises. She needed professional content captured in the moment — real footage of her doing what she does best, with her audience, in a live setting.

No staged shoots. No scripts. Just authentic content that shows who she is and what she brings to the room.

## What We Did

**Event Photography**: We captured the session as it happened — Dara leading the group, the energy in the room, the real moments that make people stop and pay attention on social media.

**Event Videography**: We shot video content throughout the session, giving Dara a library of clips she could use across her social channels. Short-form clips for Reels and TikTok, longer cuts for YouTube — all from one session.

## The Result

Dara walked away with a full library of professional content she could roll out across her social media for weeks. One event, dozens of assets. That's the kind of efficiency that makes content creation sustainable for solo entrepreneurs who can't afford to shoot every week.
