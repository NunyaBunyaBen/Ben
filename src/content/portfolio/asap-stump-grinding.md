## The Brief

ASAP Stump Grinding had just added a top-of-the-line stump grinder to their fleet. But this wasn't just any machine — they'd already nailed the branding. They called it **Clint Eatswood**. A name that good deserves a proper introduction.

The owner needed a video that matched the energy of the branding — something that announced Clint Eatswood to the world, showed what it can do, and did it with style.

## What We Did

**Launch Video**: We produced a video that introduced Clint Eatswood the way a machine with that name deserves to be introduced. Not a boring equipment demo — a proper showcase. The kind of content that makes people stop scrolling, watch the whole thing, and remember the brand.

**Facebook Ads**: We built and ran a Facebook ad campaign to put ASAP Stump Grinding in front of the right audience. Targeted, optimised, and designed to turn views into enquiries.

## Why It Worked

Sometimes a client walks in with branding so strong that your job is to match their energy and amplify it. Clint Eatswood was already a conversation starter — we just made sure the right people were part of that conversation.

Not every project needs a 12-month strategy. Sometimes you just need bold creative, smart distribution, and the confidence to let the work speak for itself.
