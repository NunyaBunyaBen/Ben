## The Vision

Bella Ryhider is a clothing brand born from one idea: what happens when 90s hip hop culture meets modern beach lifestyle? The founder had the vision — bold, free-spirited clothing that channels love, freedom, and living life on your own terms. What she didn't have was a way to bring it to life.

She's a creative entrepreneur with big ideas but no design tools, no product photography, and no online store. She needed a partner who could take the picture in her head and turn it into something real.

That's where we came in.

## What We Did

**Clothing Design**: We took her concepts and created the actual clothing designs — translating mood boards and ideas into production-ready artwork that captured the 90s-meets-beach aesthetic she was after.

**AI Photoshoot**: A traditional photoshoot with models, a photographer, a studio, and styling would have blown the budget before she'd even sold a single piece. So we did it differently. We produced a full AI photoshoot — realistic models wearing her designs in settings that matched the brand's energy. Professional-quality imagery at a fraction of the cost.

**AI Brand Video**: We went further and produced an AI video shoot as a proof of concept — giving her (and her future customers) a real feel for what the brand looks like in motion. Bold visuals, bold attitude.

**E-Commerce Website**: We built a fully functional online store ready to take orders from day one. Clean, on-brand, and built to convert browsers into buyers.

**Advertising**: We created ad creative designed to attract her target audience — people who connect with that intersection of street culture and coastal living.

## The Approach

This project was about proving that you don't need a massive budget to build a legitimate brand. Every decision was made to maximise impact while keeping costs realistic for a founder who's investing her own money.

AI-generated content wasn't the compromise — it was the strategy. It gave Bella Ryhider the professional imagery she needed to approach suppliers with confidence, test the market, and launch with a brand that looks like it's been around for years.

## Where It Stands

Bella Ryhider is fully built and launch-ready — from the designs in her head to a complete brand with product imagery, video content, a live store, and ads ready to run. Everything a clothing brand needs to hit the market and start selling.

From dream to done.
