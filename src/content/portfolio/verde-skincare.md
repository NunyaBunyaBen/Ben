## The Challenge

Verde Skincare was a brand-new Australian skincare line with zero audience, zero brand awareness, and a warehouse full of product. They needed a launch strategy that would create demand from nothing.

## What We Did

**Brand Identity**: We developed the entire visual brand — logo, packaging direction, colour palette, and brand voice. Clean, bold, and unapologetically premium.

**Pre-Launch Campaign**: Built a 6-week pre-launch email and social media campaign. Teaser content, behind-the-scenes, waitlist exclusives. We turned curiosity into commitment before a single product shipped.

**Launch Day Execution**: Coordinated email blasts, social media content, influencer drops, and retargeting ads all firing on launch day. Every touchpoint was planned to the minute.

## The Results

- **12,000+ email subscribers** before launch
- **Sold out entire first run** in 48 hours
- **2.4M social media impressions** in launch week
- **Featured in 3 major beauty publications**
