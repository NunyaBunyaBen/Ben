## The Challenge

Outback Trades is a plumbing and electrical business in regional Queensland. Great reputation locally, but their online presence was non-existent. They were losing jobs to competitors who showed up on Google — even though their work was better.

## What We Did

**Local SEO Blitz**: Optimised their Google Business Profile, built out service-area pages for every suburb they cover, and created a review generation system that tripled their Google reviews in 60 days.

**Content Strategy**: We wrote targeted service pages and blog content answering the exact questions their customers were searching for. No fluff — practical, helpful content that Google rewarded.

**Technical SEO**: Fixed site speed issues, implemented schema markup, and ensured mobile-first performance. The basics that most agencies skip.

## The Results

- **1st position** for 8 high-value local keywords
- **180% increase** in monthly enquiries
- **3x Google reviews** in 60 days
- **45% reduction** in cost per lead vs. previous ad spend
