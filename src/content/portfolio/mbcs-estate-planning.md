## The Challenge

MBCS is an estate planning law firm with a solid reputation — but their website didn't reflect it. The design was outdated, the messaging was generic, and it wasn't doing anything to build trust with potential clients before they picked up the phone.

They needed a fresh rebrand and a website that matched the calibre of their work.

## What We Did

**Website Redesign**: We rebuilt the site from the ground up with a clean, modern design that communicates authority and trust. Estate planning is personal — the website needed to feel professional without being cold, and approachable without being casual.

**Visual Rebrand**: We refreshed the visual identity to give MBCS a cohesive, polished look across their entire web presence. A brand that says "we take this seriously" before a single word is read.

**SEO Strategy**: We optimised the entire site for search — technical SEO, on-page optimisation, and content structured to rank for the terms their potential clients are actually searching for. Estate planning is a space where being found on Google is the difference between getting the call and losing it to someone else.

## The Result

MBCS now has a website that works as hard as they do. A professional online presence that builds confidence from the first click, ranks where it needs to, and gives potential clients a reason to reach out.
