## The Challenge

Coastal Fitness had a great product — boutique group training, passionate coaches, loyal members. But nobody new was finding them. Their website was buried on page 4 of Google, they had zero ad presence, and their social media was inconsistent at best.

## What We Did

**SEO Overhaul**: We rebuilt their website content from scratch, targeting high-intent local keywords. Optimised Google Business Profile, built local citations, and created a content strategy that positioned them as Brisbane's go-to boutique gym.

**Paid Ads**: Launched targeted Google Ads and Meta campaigns focused on trial memberships. We tested creative aggressively — the winning ad was one most agencies would never have run.

**Social Strategy**: Consistent, bold content that showcased real members, real results, and the personality of the gym. No stock photos. No generic motivation quotes.

## The Results

- **312% increase** in monthly leads
- **Page 1 rankings** for 15+ local keywords
- **3-month waitlist** for new memberships
- **4.2x ROAS** on paid campaigns
